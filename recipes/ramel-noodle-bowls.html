Ramel Noodle Bowls

Description

These cozy ramen noodle bowls put a fun spin on the classic noodle dish. Tender ramen noodles are topped with sautéed sweet potatoes, onions, savory sausage, and an egg for a hearty, satisfying meal that’s just as delicious for breakfast as it is for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

  • 1 sweet potato, cut into bite-sized chunks

  • salt and freshly ground black pepper as needed

  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced, divided

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh garlic

  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh ginger

  • 32 fluid ounces vegetable broth

  • 2 large eggs

  • 2 sausage patties, cooked according to package directions

  • 2 packages ramen, cooked according to package directions

  • shaved almonds, for garnish

  • dried cranberries, for garnish

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Heat vegetable oil in a cast iron skillet. Add sweet potatoes and season with a pinch each of salt and pepper. Cook potatoes, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes. Add 1/2 of the diced onions and continue to cook until potatoes are tender, 7 to 10 minutes. Remove potatoes to a plate; set aside.

  2. Step 2

    For broth, heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add garlic, ginger, and remaining onions; cook and stir for 3 to 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Pour in vegetable broth and bring to a simmer.

  3. Step 3

    Grease a ladle with cooking spray or oil, crack an egg into it, and partially submerge ladle in the simmering broth until egg white sets, then release the egg into the broth; cook undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes. Repeat with remaining egg.

  4. Step 4

    Remove eggs from broth with a ladle. Reduce heat on the broth to low and cover. Taste and adjust with salt and pepper. Remove broth from heat.

  5. Step 5

    Cook sausage according to label directions. Cook ramen according to label directions.

  6. Step 6

    Build Your Bowl: Add noodles, sausage, sweet potatoes, and egg to a bowl. Spoon broth into the bowl. Garnish with shaved almonds and cranberries.
